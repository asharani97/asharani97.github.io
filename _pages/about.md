<div class="intro-section">
  <p>
    I am a <strong>Doctoral Researcher</strong> in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at
    <a href="https://iitj.ac.in" target="_blank">IIT Jodhpur</a>, advised by
    <a href="https://yashaswi.github.io" target="_blank">Dr. Yashaswi Verma</a>.
    My thesis — <em>"Label Similarity Guided Joint Feature Learning Using Multi-View and Multi-Source Data"</em>
    (submitted August 2025) — develops unified frameworks that exploit label structure across multiple data views and modalities.
  </p>
  <p>
    I have published in Q1/Q2 journals and presented at <strong>WACV 2024</strong> (Hawaii, USA),
    supported by ANRF International Travel Support and WACV DEI Travel Grant.
  </p>
</div>

## Research Interests

<div class="research-grid">
  <div class="research-card">
    <i class="fas fa-layer-group"></i>
    <h4>Multimodal & Multi-view Learning</h4>
    <p>Joint feature learning across heterogeneous data sources and modalities using canonical correlation and contrastive methods.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="research-card">
    <i class="fas fa-tags"></i>
    <h4>Multi-label & Extreme Classification</h4>
    <p>Scalable classification over large label spaces with random feature embeddings and label-similarity guidance.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="research-card">
    <i class="fas fa-brain"></i>
    <h4>Autism Spectrum Disorder Diagnosis</h4>
    <p>Early-stage ASD detection from action/activity videos using multi-dataset contrastive learning.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="research-card">
    <i class="fas fa-photo-film"></i>
    <h4>Cross-modal Multimedia Retrieval</h4>
    <p>Dual-correlation CCA models for cross-modal matching between text and visual content.</p>
  </div>
</div>
