**Authors:** Asha Rani & Yashaswi Verma

**Venue:** *IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV) 2024* — **CORE A**

We propose a multi-dataset supervised contrastive learning framework for activity-based early diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder. By leveraging activity videos across multiple datasets, the model learns discriminative representations that generalize across data distributions.

*Presented at WACV 2024, Waikoloa, Hawaii.*
