**Authors:** Asha Rani, Rudra Dutt & Yashaswi Verma

**Venue:** *International Journal of Data Science and Analytics*, 21 (1), 27 (2026) — **Q2 Journal**

We show that random feature embeddings provide strong, computationally efficient baselines for extreme multi-label text classification, outperforming several complex methods on benchmark datasets.
